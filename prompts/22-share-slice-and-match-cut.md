# Phase 22 of 24: The match cut and the slice

You are improving orange.io. Phase 21 retimed The Model into four beats. This phase makes the last two moves of the chapter read as filmed, not switched: the grove → share cut must be a match cut on the gilded orange, and inside the share beat the orange must be sliced by a front that runs along the knife's line, not crossfaded or run through the generic static treatment. It is specified in `docs/TRANSITIONS.md` (row Graft → grove → share) and `docs/STORY.md` (1 · The Model); build to the spec.

## What the visitor sees

The picking hand closes round the gilded orange. The picture around it changes (the canopy gives way to flat paper-white and a young man in green velvet with auburn curls) while the fruit stays exactly where it is on screen, same place and same size, so the eye rides the orange across the cut. He holds it up and studies it; then his knife meets it and a thin, ragged front runs along the blade's line through the fruit, and behind that front the orange is two halves, held a hand's width apart by two hands, the second having come in from the left. That held frame is the one The Split opens on.

## Task

1. **Match cut.** `art.grove.gilded` (the fruit in the grove) and `art.share.fruit` (the fruit in the share, which the last keyframe shares) are the two anchors. Through the grove → share cut, drive both sides with `uncover()` so the gilded orange lands at the same screen position and scale on both sides at the cut's centre, at every viewport (1440x900, 1920x1080, an ultrawide 2560x1080, a tablet and the 390x844 phone band). Do it by solving the pan and zoom each side needs at the cut (blended in and out over the cut's width, with no jump and no velocity kink) rather than by nudging the painting; the share's own camera then eases away from that framing to its resting `lastFraming` as before. Inside a soft disc around the fruit (radius from `art.ts`, scaled by the fruit's size) scale the cut's tear, mosaic and chroma split to zero so the fruit reads as the one thing that does not change; every pixel is still one picture or the other. Add a test that the fruit's centre differs by at most 1 px between the two sides at the cut's midpoint at all five sizes.
2. **The slice.** Replace the generic static treatment for the share beat's keyframe cut with a purpose-built front. Add the knife's line to `art.share` as a point and an angle in the painting's own units, and the front's travel direction along it. The front is a thin band, bitten by `fbm` (rule 1) and printed with a Bayer and stipple edge (rule 2); ahead of it is the study keyframe, behind it the halves-apart keyframe. Behind the front the second picture is still catching up (the same lag mechanism the Split uses: `exp(-distBehindCut / d)`, a small offset along the front's direction, 5-tap smear along the way it moves, rule 6) and the lag is exactly 0 at the end so the beat comes to rest on the keyframe. Give the front's leading edge a thin pale glint (cream, 1 to 2 px × dpr) and a few hashed zest-coloured specks on the cut (rule 10, per cell and per sixth of a second). Lengthen the cut to read as a slice, not a flash (about 150 scroll units, set in `timeline.ts` after the typed line has finished), and keep the copy legible in column A: the front must not pass over it. Fast paths: pixels provably ahead of or behind the front, and outside the band, are one texture tap and grain; the pass must not get slower anywhere the front is not.
3. **Expose and tune.** Put every number behind a `tune` object the HUD shows, with sensible defaults, and add `?model=<units>` (or reuse the existing `?p=` with `clock=5`) to hold the cut at any point. Look at the real frames at t = 0.2, 0.5 and 0.8 of the slice and tune by eye until the front reads as a knife's path, the edge is ragged and printed, and no frame reads as a crossfade.
4. **Prove it.** Screenshots at 1440x900 and 390x844 into `docs/screenshots/model-slice-*.png` and `docs/screenshots/model-match-*.png` (before, middle and after the match cut, with a marker at the fruit's centre drawn by the test, not in the product). Scrub the share beat forward and back at a fixed clock and confirm identical frames; confirm the first frame of the beat is the study and the last is `shareLast` exactly; run the Split test and confirm t = 0 diffs 0. The model pass costs under 8 ms at 1440x900 during the slice with the fast paths on. Lint, typecheck and build green; update `docs/TRANSITIONS.md` with the final numbers and `docs/CHECKS.md` with the new tests.
