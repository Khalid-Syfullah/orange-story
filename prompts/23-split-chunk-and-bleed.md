# Phase 23 of 24: The Split, dots and bleed

You are improving orange.io. Phase 6 built The Split from the first spec (a noise-bitten vertical seam, pith, zest, rind, the plate arriving lagged). The spec now adds two dot treatments that make it the page's signature: the cobalt does not wipe in, it **bleeds** outward from the seam as a coarse halftone, dots that grow until they merge; and the halves and hands that are pushed aside **pixelate** into chunky dots as they leave. `docs/TRANSITIONS.md` has the full spec (the `chunk` and `bleed` rows of the parameter table, and the skeleton); build to it.

## What the visitor sees

With the halves held a hand's width apart, the gap tears open. Cobalt arrives from the seam through a grainy halftone and stipple edge; the dots grow until they merge into flat blue. The halves and hands are pushed aside, smearing, and then breaking into chunky dots as they go. A white pith rim runs along the cut and a few zest specks spray from it. The page settles on flat cobalt with one small halftone cloud, ready for the first Work card.
