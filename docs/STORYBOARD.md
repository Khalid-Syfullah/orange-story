# Orange: storyboard

The whole site is one continuous scroll story in four chapters, plus a finale. Everything below happens on a single tall, pinned stage: the art changes, the camera moves, and text appears and leaves in set places as the visitor scrolls. Scrolling up always plays the story backwards.

## Visual style

- Painterly, classical figures (robes, marble, timber scaffolds) in a warm, sunlit world; deep blue skies; milk-white pages; oranges as the one strong accent.
- Hairline grid lines, small star-shaped marks where they cross, a chapter ruler on the left, tiny uppercase labels, light serif headlines.
- Movement is slow and deliberate: long camera moves, gentle sway, soft particles.

## Scene 1. The hero: the raised orange

**Chapter:** The Model. **Frame:** wide, still, painterly.

- A clear, deep blue sky fills the screen.
- Upper right: a classical statue of a woman stands on top of a slender minaret. Her right arm is raised high, holding an orange up to the sky like a torch.
- Light spills from the orange and spreads across the sky as a slow ripple of glowing droplets, like rain in reverse. This plays continuously, even before scrolling.
- Lower right, at the foot of the minaret: a small crowd of robed figures in classical dress, all turning their faces up toward the statue.
- Center left: the hero title, with the subtitle beneath it and the "Start a partnership" pill button under that.
- Bottom left: the "What we do" tag, and one tab-width to its right, the short paragraph.
- Chrome: mark and grid button top left, "Apply" top right, the ruler down the left edge, thin hairline grid lines.

## Scene 2. The ascent: the minaret becomes a tree

**Chapter:** The Model.

- As scrolling begins, the orange's glow brightens toward white and the droplet ripple speeds up a little.
- The title, subtitle and button drift left and fade out (staggered, title first).
- The camera pushes in on the statue. While it pushes in, the minaret keeps rising, as if it is growing taller and taller.
- The stone of the minaret slowly turns into bark: the shaft becomes a tree trunk, branches split off, and leaves unfurl.
- The push-in eases into a slow pan and dolly along the trunk. By the end the camera is steady, framing a single branch with leaves.

## Scene 3. The graft

**Chapter:** The Model.

- Two hands enter the frame. They are painted in the same classical style as the statue and the crowd, with robe sleeves at the wrists.
- They graft a cutting onto the branch: fit the cutting to a cleft, wrap it, and tie it off with a neat knot of twine.
- When the knot is tied, the camera dollies up and pulls out. The tree fills with oranges among the leaves, swaying gently left and right.

## Scene 4. The pluck and the harvester

**Chapter:** The Model.

- A hand rises from the bottom center of the screen toward one special orange in the middle of the frame.
- As the hand closes on the orange, the first beat appears center left, slightly low: title "We get you found." with its subtitle. At the same moment a thin progress line runs left to right along the hairline grid.
- The camera dollies down through the branches, past many oranges, and the hand pulls the fruit free.
- The harvester is revealed: a man in a white classical robe, holding the orange in his right hand and looking at it.
- Bottom left, a second beat: title "We split the harvest." with the small "Revenue share" tag under it (decorative, not a button that does anything). To the right of the tag, a line types itself out letter by letter.

## Scene 5. The cut and the share

**Chapter:** The Model, ending.

- His left hand comes in with a knife and cuts through the orange he is still holding in his right.
- The camera pushes into the orange; the beat text fades out.
- The orange opens into two halves. A second person's hand reaches in from center left and takes one half. For a moment both hands hold the halves together: the harvest, shared.
- The thin hairline grid draws itself into a square frame around the halves, like a camera viewfinder.
- Camera pushes into the halves. Transition: the screen fills with blue, and drifting grains like fine sand sweep across and cover everything as the user scrolls. The ruler switches to "The Work".

## Scene 6. The work: services in the clouds

**Chapter:** The Work.

- Blue sky with soft clouds. Clouds drift from the center toward the right; the camera dollies slowly upward.
- Right side of the screen, center right: the service cards appear one at a time, each as a small tag, a title, and a body paragraph. Card 1, then card 2 (as clouds pass on both sides), then card 3.
- During card 3 a very large orange rises into view on top of a minaret, with a builder in classical dress working on it.
- The ruler on the left shows "The Work" as active. Hovering the ruler reveals all four chapters.
- A scroll percentage counter is visible in the bottom-right area.

## Scene 7. The blueprint

**Chapter:** The Work, ending.

- The minaret is under construction, wrapped in timber scaffolding.
- On the scaffold platforms: the builder at work, and an architect in a white robe and head covering holding a folded drawing of the minaret.
- The architect unfolds the drawing panel by panel, and the camera pushes into the plan as it opens.
- Transition: the plan fills the frame and everything turns milk white, then the next scene unfolds out of the white like paper opening. The ruler switches to "The Terms".

## Scene 8. The building site by the sea

**Chapter:** The Terms.

- An artistic, painterly scene: level ground beside the sea. On a low marble platform, a large orange is being built.
- Three builders: one on a small timber stage to the left of the orange, one on the right, and one seated on a stool on the stage holding the plan. A short flight of wooden steps leads down to the ground.
- The camera rises from ground level to a straight top-down view: the crown of the orange, the marble, the timber stage, the tops of the builders' heads. No sky.
- Keep scrolling and the top-down view zooms out and shrinks into the left half of the screen. The pattern grows into a large field of tiles around the scaffold.
- The right half becomes a calm milk-white panel with very soft, blurred shadows of leaves moving across it.
- In the right half: the terms title, the body, then the "In plain terms" tag with the side text to its right.

## Scene 9. The gardener

**Chapter:** The Terms.

- Transition from the bottom up: blue rising with small white specks, like dust lifting in light, until it covers the screen.
- A gardener in classical dress stands center on a minaret-like column under blue sky. On top of the column is a white marble urn with a young orange tree in it, a few small oranges on its branches. He trims the branches to shape it.
- The large chapter title "The Terms" rises from the bottom in very large type, travels upward, and shrinks as it goes until it settles in the top-left corner, inside the margin and never crossing the ruler.
- At the bottom: the fit text, then the "In plain terms" tag with the side text to its right.

## Scene 10. The falling orange

**Chapter:** The Terms, ending.

- A cut branch drops from the tree. The camera moves toward the gardener's face, then he drifts away to the right and out of frame.
- An orange from the cut branch falls toward the ground. The camera follows and pushes into it until it fills the center.
- All the text blurs and sinks into the background.
- Transition: a circular rainbow ring sweeps outward from the orange and changes the whole background around it.
- The orange, still falling, goes soft and out of focus. Behind it, orange trees line the left and right, branches heavy with fruit, sky in the center. The camera dollies upward between the rows.
- The ruler switches to "Questions", and "Questions worth asking first." appears at the top center.

## Scene 11. The questions

**Chapter:** Questions.

- Each question is a card: question as the title, answer inside the card.
- A thin white connector line ties each card to an orange in the trees. One end is fixed on the orange and never moves; the other end is a small square that stays locked to a corner of the card as the card moves. Lines run at angles of about 45 degrees.
- Card 1 zooms up from small to full size, its title arriving first, then the answer. Two small sparkle stars twinkle in its corner.
- Scrolling on: card 1 swings left along a curve, blurs and shrinks, and card 2 arrives from the right into the center. Its line anchors on a different orange and locks to a different corner of the card.
- Cards 3, 4 and 5 follow the same way, each with its own anchor orange (alternating left and right rows of trees) and its own card corner.
- Scrolling back reverses everything exactly: earlier cards come back the way they left.
- After the last card, all text blurs and dissolves upward like smoke.

## Scene 12. The stargazer

**Chapter:** Questions.

- The camera moves forward into the blue between the trees, a cloud at the top center, the trees sliding away. The sky becomes soft, like fabric, and a cloud billows over to fill the screen. Then blue again.
- A new figure: an astronomer in classical dress stands top left on a marble platform with marble stairs descending toward the viewer. He points to the sky with his right hand; his finger is clear.
- Stars appear and spread across the sky like a growing graph: white nodes, some joined by thin lines, many just twinkling. The bottom right stays clear: only clouds there.

## Scene 13. The application

**Chapter:** Questions.

- Top left: the small "Apply" tag (a transparent chip with white text), then the application title and subtitle under it.
- Right half: the form. Row one holds two short fields side by side, "Your name" and "Work email". Row two holds the larger "What should grow?" field. Then the "Send application" button.
- The fields are clear glass: almost fully transparent, with a soft edge highlight. Each field label has a small icon.
- On hover or focus, the active field lifts forward and the others ease back and down, in a gentle 3D stack.

## Scene 14. The finale: the orchard keepers

**Chapter:** End.

- The astronomer and his marble platform sink away toward the bottom. Clouds drift from left to right. The form blurs, rises and fades out. The stars drift up and away.
- From the bottom center a tree rises into view, its branches filling the frame.
- A woman with a watering can comes in and waters the tree. A man comes in from the right with shears in both hands and trims stray branches.
- They settle on either side of the tree, which stands in the center.
- This now plays as a continuous loop that no longer depends on scrolling: she waters, he cuts an orange free, oranges drop now and then, the clouds drift right.
- The hairline grid closes in from the edges toward the center to form a camera-style frame. Inside it: the "Orange" wordmark in the boldest type on the site, the subtitle below it, and at the bottom the copyright line and the contact email.
- This is the end of the page: further scrolling does nothing. Scrolling back up plays everything in reverse, all the way to the hero.
